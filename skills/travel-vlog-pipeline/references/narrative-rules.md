# Narrative Rules

## Rule Ownership

The three-act rhythm, 3-4 second advisory pace, travel content ratios, recommended source ranges, and speech-boundary guidance come from the pinned Vlog rules source. The restaurant-specific arrival-to-exit taxonomy below is this integration's profile and must not be attributed to upstream.

## Food Vlog Story Roles

Preferred order:

1. `arrival`: route, arrival, or first approach.
2. `exterior`: storefront, sign, queue, or neighborhood context.
3. `entry`: entering, greeting, or transition indoors.
4. `environment`: room, seating, atmosphere, menu overview.
5. `ordering`: ordering, price, menu choice, or anticipation.
6. `serving`: dishes arriving, uncovering, pouring, or plating.
7. `food_hero`: clear dish overview and detail.
8. `food_action`: steam, cutting, mixing, dipping, lifting, or bite preparation.
9. `tasting_reaction`: tasting, reaction, texture, flavor, or value judgment.
10. `summary_exit`: conclusion, recommendation, bill, leaving, or exterior ending.

Coverage is a review gate, not a license to fabricate. When footage lacks a role, report it as a gap and ask whether the user accepts the omission.

## Travel Vlog Story Roles

Preferred order:

1. `transit`
2. `arrival`
3. `scenic_transition`
4. `environment`
5. `people_interaction`
6. `food_hero` or another main activity
7. `summary_exit`

Keep the chronological route unless a clearly reviewed hook needs a brief reordering.

## Pacing

- Treat 3-4 seconds per visual shot as an advisory center, not a hard rule.
- Use sub-2-second shots only in a deliberate montage.
- Allow shots over 8 seconds when continuous speech, action, or emotional payoff needs them.
- Alternate wide, medium, close-up, and action/reaction coverage.
- Avoid repeating the same source moment merely to satisfy a ratio.
- For visual-only material, warn above 8 seconds unless sustained action justifies it.

## Content Balance

Use ratios as diagnostic warnings:

- food: 30-45%
- scenery/environment: around 30%
- people/interaction: around 15%
- daily/transit: around 15%

For a restaurant review, actual dish coverage may exceed the travel reference range when the user wants detail. Keep enough environment and people to preserve place and experience.

## Food Motion

Use restrained center-weighted push-ins only when a food shot is stable, at least 3.5 seconds, and visually benefits from emphasis. Default zoom is 1.08. Do not add markers, whip motion, synthetic camera paths, or motion that implies detail outside the source frame.

## Speech Boundaries

- Round plan cuts to 0.1 seconds.
- If a boundary falls inside speech, expand to 0.1 seconds before the sentence and 0.2 seconds after it.
- Clamp repaired cuts to source duration and validate again.
- Preserve natural room tone around reactions when it contributes to the visit.

## Music and Narration

- Add BGM only after identifying a real pacing or emotional gap.
- Duck BGM under meaningful speech.
- Add AI narration only when essential context cannot be recovered from existing speech, titles, or visuals.
- Never use narration to claim a dish, price, location, or reaction not evidenced by the footage or user brief.
